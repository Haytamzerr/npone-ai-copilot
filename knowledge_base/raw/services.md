# Our Services

N+ONE DATACENTERS provides the full range of infrastructure and support solutions businesses need
to thrive in today's digital landscape.

## Infrastructure Hosting

Digital infrastructure hosting solutions ensuring high availability, security, and scalability.
From virtual servers to dedicated hardware, hosting environments are tailored to meet the diverse
needs of modern enterprises, so clients can focus on their core business without worrying about
uptime or performance.

## Turnkey Datacenters

Fully customized design and deployment of enterprise-grade facilities, delivered ready for use.
N+ONE handles everything from site planning and electrical engineering to rack installation and
final commissioning. The datacenter is built to the client's exact specifications, minimizing
time-to-market and maximizing operational efficiency.

## Hyperscale Datacenters

Purpose-built infrastructures tailored to the exacting demands of global hyperscalers. Designed
for massive workloads and seamless expansion, offering the power, cooling, and network
connectivity required by large-scale cloud providers, content platforms, and AI workloads.

## Datacenter Services

- Colocation in carrier-neutral facilities with redundant power and cooling.
- Disaster recovery fallback space stocked with essential IT and office equipment to minimize
  downtime.
- Managed services covering monitoring, patch management, and maintenance to keep infrastructure
  secure and up to date.

## Connectivity

Carrier-neutral connectivity offering seamless access to major carriers, ISPs, Internet
Exchanges, and CDNs. Multiple transit and peering options reduce latency and eliminate single
points of failure.

## Business Continuity

Comprehensive business continuity and disaster recovery services, featuring dedicated fallback
space equipped with essential IT and office equipment. In the event of a disruption (natural
disaster, power outage, cyber incident), a client's team can continue operations without missing
a beat, thanks to ready-to-use recovery sites.

## Why Choose N+ONE

1. **Proven expertise** — decades of combined experience in designing, building, and operating
   mission-critical datacenters.
2. **Hands & Eyes** — engineers provide Smart Hands services to handle daily data center tasks
   like rack & stack, troubleshooting, and equipment management.
3. **Managed Connectivity** — a 24/7 IT team offers a full range of Managed Services, plus custom
   solutions tailored to client needs.
4. **Business Continuity** — all facilities are fitted with premium offices and spaces for
   disaster recovery purposes, available on a 24/7 basis.
