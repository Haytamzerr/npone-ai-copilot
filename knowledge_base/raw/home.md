# About N+ONE DATACENTERS

N+ONE DATACENTERS is a leader in hyperscale data center infrastructure and colocation across
Africa, delivering resilient and scalable infrastructures to address the mission-critical demands
of modern enterprises. Its facilities are equipped with advanced technology and innovative designs
to drive sustainable, technology-driven progress across the continent.

N+ONE DATACENTERS offers a comprehensive ecosystem designed to facilitate digital transformation
and ensure operational continuity for businesses of all sizes.

## Core Offering

- **Infrastructure Hosting**: digital infrastructure hosting solutions ensuring high availability,
  security, and scalability, tailored to the diverse needs of modern enterprises.
- **Turnkey Data Centers**: fully customized design and deployment of enterprise-grade facilities,
  delivered ready for use.
- **Hyperscale Data Centers**: purpose-built infrastructures tailored to the exacting demands of
  global hyperscalers.
- **Data Center Services**: full suite of colocation, disaster recovery, and managed solutions.
- **Connectivity**: seamless carrier-neutral connectivity to major carriers, ISPs, Internet
  Exchanges, and CDNs.
- **Business Continuity**: disaster recovery services providing fallback space with essential IT
  and office equipment for quick recovery.

## AI Ready Datacenter

Specialized processors (GPUs, TPUs) push rack power densities far beyond the 3-20 kW range found in
traditional facilities, with sustained loads that can exceed 50-100 kW. The environment is
engineered to support continuous training, large-scale inference, and the demanding thermal and
electrical requirements of modern AI systems.

- **Flexible GPU Access**: deploy GPUs on-premises or through the N+ONE cloud platform, depending
  on workload needs.
- **Morocco-Local AI Services**: AI capabilities delivered directly from a local region, ensuring
  low-latency access and in-country data handling.
- **Full AI Readiness**: purpose-built HPC and AI/ML hosting supported by leading technology
  integrators.

## Locations / Campuses

- **STT Campus** — Settat, Morocco: excellent transport and connectivity links, ideal for
  businesses needing easy access to major Moroccan cities from a secure, centralized location.
- **CMN Campus** — Casablanca, Morocco: stable environment for business operations, away from
  coastal risks, close to the country's main commercial and industrial centers.
- **ABJ Campus** — Abidjan, Ivory Coast: key economic center in West Africa, dynamic business
  environment with great connectivity.
- **DKR Campus** — Dakar, Senegal: major hub for West Africa, excellent regional connectivity,
  strategic position for expansion into African and global markets.

N+ONE DATACENTERS currently operates in three countries: Morocco, Senegal, and Ivory Coast.

## Sustainability Commitment

- **Renewable Energy**: N+ONE DATACENTERS will source 100% of its energy from renewable sources
  through a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA).
- **Carbon Neutrality**: committed to achieving carbon neutrality by 2027.
- **Energy-Efficient Data Centers**: facilities maintain a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) between
  1.03 and 1.3.
- **Optimized Cooling Systems**: at least 120 days of free cooling each year.

## Certifications & International Standards

- **ATD® (Accredited Tier Designer)** — reliable and efficient data center designs.
- **Tier III Facility** — reliable and redundant infrastructure guaranteeing uptime and
  operational resilience.
- **PCI DSS** — secure environment for sensitive payment card data.
- **ISO 14001** — environmental management systems for sustainable operations.
- **ISO 27001** — certified Information Security Management System (ISMS) for systematic data
  protection.
- **HDS (Hébergeur de Données de Santé)** — certified Health Data Hosting, ensuring security,
  confidentiality, and compliance of sensitive personal health information.
